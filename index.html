Climbing Gym Tycoon

Build the bouldering gym

What it is

Run a climbing gym from one bare unit to a place people plan their week around. Put the walls up yourself, bolt the holds on route by route, and sign the first climbers in at the desk in person — then earn the setters, the build crew and the receptionist who take those jobs off you.

Routes go stale and the regulars notice. A wall too hard for the room empties out. The cafe earns from people who never climb. Take the unit next door when you outgrow this one, then the high bay when you outgrow that.

An early build, made by one person

Most of it works; some of it is rough, and a few things are missing. If you find a bug or think of something the game should do, that is genuinely useful — there is a feedback route on the first screen of the app and another in Settings.
