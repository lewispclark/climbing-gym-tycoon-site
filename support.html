Things worth knowing before you write

  • Your gym is saved on your device only. There is no account and no copy on a server, so a gym lost with a deleted app cannot be restored — by me or by anyone.
  • If the game said your save could not be read, the damaged file was kept rather than deleted. Say so in your message; it may be recoverable in a later version.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Refunds go through reportaproblem.apple.com — I cannot issue them, and I never see your payment details.
  • Telling me your device and iOS version saves a round trip.

What this game does with your information

Nothing leaves your device. The privacy policy is the long version, and it is written against what the build actually does.
